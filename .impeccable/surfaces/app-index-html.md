## Scope

Start screen (Stage 0) and the vault chrome (SORT / FILE TYPES under the wordmark) of `app/index.html`. Mode: Persuade for the start screen (the one action is ENTER ARKHIVE); Operate for the vault chrome.

## Audience and job

One owner on their own Mac. Job: get into their files, or learn in one press (DETAILS) what Arkhive can read and what macOS blocks. Proof is the owner's real counts only; nothing invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the archive is a calm place you walk into, not a dashboard. Refuses the category default of a dark hero with product screenshot and feature grid.
OWN-WORLD: pixel-art meadow at 4 CSS px per art pixel in the pinned palette only (#EDE6DA, #F5EFE6, #D8CFC0, #261A14, #5C463B, #8A7367, #234434, #3A634C, #B89658); ordered dither, no gradients, no blur. Inter Tight 800 wordmark, Newsreader for sentences, bold uppercase boxed labels, hairline 1px rules.
STORY: ARKHIVE, one sentence, one forest-green ENTER ARKHIVE with a hard mocha offset (owner's brief), DETAILS for live facts, the laptop on the grass showing files in motion.
FIRST VIEWPORT: wordmark centred; laptop and spiral notebook bottom-right on the meadow; oak framing the right edge; skyline left of centre; path, bench, gold wildflower.
